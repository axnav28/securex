## Product story

SecureX turns cyber risk into a defensible financial decision.

Recommended flow:

1. Landing page: establish the board-level problem.
2. Dashboard: show expected annual loss and evidence confidence.
3. Simulator: toggle a control and show the model react.
4. Attack graph: trace root cause and downstream exposure.
5. Optimizer: compare reduction, cost, and return.
6. Reports or compliance: show traceability and governance.
